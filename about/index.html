About Byte Sized Academy

Big topics explained simply, in one minute or less.

What we make

Byte Sized Academy is a free, one-minute classroom. Each short video explains one idea, simply, in about a minute. Small ideas stack up, so a series of one-minute lessons can take you from "what is a network?" all the way to exam-level topics.

Every lesson also gets its own page on this site, with a little more detail, the key words to know, something quick to try, and the sources behind every fact.

What we teach

We're a general education channel. Our first series is Network Bytes, which teaches computer networking from zero and follows the Cisco CCNA exam topics. More subjects are on the way.

How lessons are made

Lessons are written and produced with help from AI tools, including the narration voice and the animation. Every script is checked against credible sources, which are listed under each lesson, and every video is reviewed by a person before it's posted. If you spot a mistake, please tell us and we'll fix it.

Where to watch

New lessons post on Instagram at @bytesizedacademyeducation, and they all live here on the lessons page.

Support free learning

Byte Sized Academy is free, with no ads and no paywall. If our lessons help you, you can support the channel with a tip on Ko-fi. Every bit goes toward making more lessons.
